Metacheck reports for PsyArXiv preprints

Metacheck screens scientific manuscripts to identify potential issues or areas for improvement and guide researchers in adopting best practices. It is a module based open source tool. For information on the available modules, see the manual. This page shows Metacheck's reports on preprints posted on PsyArXiv. The reports are automated: they miss things and flag things that are fine, so read every finding as a question, not a verdict. If you find an error, please leave an issue on GitHub. You can run Metacheck on your own paper on our server. We thank SURF for SURF Research Cloud, on which these checks run.
