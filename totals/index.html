Totals

Every number on this page is counted by Metacheck, which is automated: it flags things that are fine, and misses things. A count of problems is a count of things to check, not of mistakes. The preprints are those Metacheck has checked so far, not a sample of PsyArXiv chosen at random. The numbers are counted again once a day.
